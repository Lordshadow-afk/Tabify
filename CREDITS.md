# Credits and references

Everything here, including every mascot, is drawn or written from scratch for this project. Where a look is inspired by
something else, only the general mood is borrowed. No artwork, characters, music, fonts or writing are copied.
Nothing here is affiliated with or endorsed by the works named.

- **CRT flicker.** The idea comes from "CRT Screen Text Flicker Animation In Pure CSS" on CodeMyUI (Saijo George, January 2020),
  which shows a CodePen by Patrick H. Lauke. No license is stated for that pen, so none of its code is used. The scanlines, rolling band,
  color fringing and timing are written from scratch.
- **Retro RPG box style.** A fan homage to the look of [Undertale](https://undertale.com) by Toby Fox: black boxes, thick white frames, a heart pointer.
- **Tangerine theme.** A homage to the mood of the Korean drama *When Life Gives You Tangerines* (Netflix, 2025): love, joy and sorrow arriving together.
- **Ryōmen theme.** Based on the old legend in the [Nihon Shoki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nihon_Shoki) (720 CE), a man with one body and two faces.
  See [Yokai.jp](https://yokai.jp/en/yokai/ryomen-sukuna) and W. G. Aston's 1896 [Nihongi translation](https://archive.org/details/nihongichronicl00astogoog) (public domain).
  The manga ink style takes its general mood from dark-fantasy manga such as Jujutsu Kaisen by Gege Akutami.
- **Ocean Glass theme and halftone poster style.** Follow the general look of vintage screen-printed character posters.
- **Crimson Styx theme.** A homage to the look of [Hades](https://www.supergiantgames.com/games/hades/) by Supergiant Games: near-black ground, crimson and gold, embers.
- **Three-headed hound.** The myth is ancient: Hesiod's [Theogony](https://cts.perseids.org/read/greekLit/tlg0020/tlg001/perseus-eng2/290-390)
  and [Theoi](https://theoi.com/Ther/KuonKerberos.html). The drawing is original.
- **Greek key border.** An ancient motif, see [Meander (art)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meander_(art)). Drawn from scratch.
- **Flicker safety.** Follows [WCAG 2.3.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/three-flashes-or-below-threshold.html) (dips of 6% or less),
  can be switched off, and stops under [prefers-reduced-motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-motion).
