Search box text

Shown in the search box before you type. Leave it empty to use the default. Each theme can have its own.

Box style

Retro RPG: thick white frames and a heart that points at the link you hover. Classical Greek: double gold frames, a Greek-key border and drifting embers. Manga ink: heavy borders with hard red shadows, halftone dots and focus lines. Halftone poster: a navy ground, cream panels, heavy type and printed-dot art.

Effects

Scanlines, a slow rolling band and a gentle low-contrast flicker. It never flashes brightly and switches off if your system asks for reduced motion.

Your themes

Themes are plain JSON. Colors must be #rrggbb, images must be https addresses, and your uploaded images never travel inside one.

Each block is one card. The first line is the card name. Every line after it is a link:
Label | address | key
The key is optional. Press it on the page to open that link. Leave a blank line between cards.

Keep my settings safe

Clearing cookies and site data in Firefox or Chrome wipes anything a web page saves. Your settings live in two more places that survive it. Use the first one and you never have to think about this again.

1. Your personal link. Set it as your New Tab Override address (or bookmark it). Your whole setup is inside the link, so it comes back by itself after a wipe and syncs with your bookmarks.
2. A backup file. Includes your uploaded images, which a link cannot hold.
3. Ask the browser to keep it. Stops automatic clean-ups when your disk is low. It cannot stop you clearing the data yourself.

References and credits
  • CRT text flicker. The look comes from the idea in "CRT Screen Text Flicker Animation In Pure CSS" on CodeMyUI (article by Saijo George, January 2020), which shows a CodePen by Patrick H. Lauke. No license is stated for that pen, so none of its code is used here. The scanlines, rolling band, color fringing and flicker timing in this project are written from scratch.
  • Retro RPG box style. A fan-made homage to the look of Undertale by Toby Fox: black boxes with thick white frames, a heart that points at your choice, and dialogue-style text starting with "*". Not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's creator. No game art, characters, music, writing or fonts are used. The heart is a plain pixel shape drawn for this project, and the page uses your system's monospace font.
  • Tangerine theme. A fan homage to the bittersweet mood of the Korean drama When Life Gives You Tangerines (Netflix, 2025), set on Jeju Island, where love, joy and sorrow arrive together. Only that mood is borrowed: warm tangerine orange, soft cream, a serif face, and an original fruit mascot that smiles and cries at the same time. No characters, scenes, posters, logos or text from the show are used, and the project is not affiliated with it. The search placeholder line was supplied by the project owner and can be changed in Customize.
  • Ryōmen theme. The figure comes from an old legend. The Nihon Shoki (720 CE), in its entry for the 65th year of Emperor Nintoku, describes a man of Hida Province with one body and two faces set back to back, with hands and feet for each, swords at both sides and a bow used with four hands. See Yokai.jp: Ryōmen Sukuna for the legend and later folklore, and W. G. Aston's 1896 Nihongi translation (public domain). The specter here is an original drawing of that legend: two faces on one head, four arms with a bow, an arrow and two swords, and a body that fades away.
  • Ocean Glass theme and Halftone poster style. The navy-and-cream palette, printed halftone dots, bold title type and cream-framed picture follow the general look of vintage screen-printed character posters. The sleepy seal is an original drawing for this project; no characters, artwork or text from any franchise are used, and the project is not affiliated with any. The search placeholder is the project's own line.
  • Manga ink style. Heavy black ink, halftone dots, hard red shadows and focus lines are the general look of dark-fantasy manga, with the mood inspired by Jujutsu Kaisen by Gege Akutami. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the author or publisher. No artwork, character designs or writing from the series are used, and the series' character is not drawn. The one quotation is the search box line, chosen by the project owner; it can be changed or cleared in Customize, Theme.
  • Crimson Styx theme. A fan homage to the look of Hades by Supergiant Games: a near-black ground, crimson and gold, double gold frames, a Greek-key border and rising embers. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the studio. No game art, characters, music, writing or fonts are used.
  • The three-headed hound. The myth is ancient and in the public domain: Hesiod's Theogony (about 700 BCE) names Cerberus, and Theoi gathers the ancient sources. The game's own character designs are not public domain, so the hound here is an original drawing made for this project: a standing dog in side view with three necks, bold dark outlines and flat color, in the theme's crimson and gold. It is not the game's version of the character.
  • Greek key border. The repeating pattern is an ancient decorative motif (see Meander (art)). The version used here is drawn from scratch.
  • Flicker safety. The flicker follows the limits in WCAG 2.3.1, Three Flashes or Below Threshold (dips of 6% or less, well under three per second), can be switched off, and stops when prefers-reduced-motion is on.
